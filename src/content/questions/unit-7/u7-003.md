stem: |
  A 0.25 kg toy hangs from a spring and bobs up and down 4.0 times every second.
  The spring constant of the spring is closest to which of the following?
choices:
  A: '6.3 N/m'
  B: '25 N/m'
  C: '160 N/m'
  D: '630 N/m'
answer: C
why_wrong:
  A: 'That is $m\omega$. In $k = m\omega^2$ the angular frequency is squared.'
  B: 'That squares only the frequency, giving $2\pi m f^2$. The whole angular frequency, $2\pi f$, must be squared.'
  D: 'That is $\omega^2$ by itself. It still needs to be multiplied by the mass, 0.25 kg.'
---

"4.0 times every second" is the frequency: $f = 4.0$ Hz. For a mass on a spring,

$$
T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}}
$$

which rearranges to

$$
k = m\left(\frac{2\pi}{T}\right)^2 = m (2\pi f)^2
$$

With $2\pi f = 2\pi (4.0\ \text{Hz}) \approx 25\ \text{rad/s}$:

$$
k \approx (0.25\ \text{kg})(25\ \text{s}^{-1})^2 \approx 160\ \text{N/m}
$$
