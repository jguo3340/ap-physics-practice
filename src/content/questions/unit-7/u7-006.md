stem: |
  A motion sensor records the position $x$ of a cart oscillating on a spring,
  measured from equilibrium; part of the data is shown. What total distance
  does the cart travel between $t = 0.20$ s and $t = 0.60$ s?
choices:
  A: '0 cm'
  B: '5 cm'
  C: '10 cm'
  D: '20 cm'
answer: C
why_wrong:
  A: 'That is the displacement: the cart is back at $x = 0$ at 0.60 s. But it moved the whole time, out to $-5$ cm and back.'
  B: 'That counts only the trip out to $-5$ cm. The cart then travels 5 cm back to equilibrium by 0.60 s.'
  D: 'That is the distance for a full cycle, which takes 0.80 s. From 0.20 s to 0.60 s is only half a cycle.'
---

Read the graph at the three key moments:

- At $t = 0.20$ s the cart passes through $x = 0$, moving in the negative direction.
- At $t = 0.40$ s it reaches $x = -5$ cm and turns around.
- At $t = 0.60$ s it is back at $x = 0$.

Distance adds up every bit of travel, whatever the direction: $5\ \text{cm} + 5\ \text{cm} = 10\ \text{cm}$. The displacement over the same interval is zero, which is why 0 cm is a tempting wrong answer.
