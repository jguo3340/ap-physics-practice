stem: |
  Four springs, P, Q, R, and S, hang from the ceiling, each with a block
  attached. The table gives each block's mass and how far each spring is
  stretched when its block hangs at rest.

  $\begin{array}{c|c|c} \text{Spring} & \text{Mass (kg)} & \text{Stretch (cm)} \\ \hline P & 0.20 & 8.0 \\ Q & 0.10 & 6.0 \\ R & 0.40 & 4.0 \\ S & 0.25 & 5.0 \end{array}$

  Each block is pulled down a small distance and released. Which one oscillates
  with the longest period?
choices:
  A: 'The block on spring P'
  B: 'The block on spring Q'
  C: 'The block on spring R'
  D: 'The block on spring S'
answer: A
why_wrong:
  B: 'The block on spring Q is the lightest, but the period depends on the ratio of mass to spring constant. Spring Q stretches less than spring P, so its period is shorter.'
  C: 'The block on spring R is the heaviest, but spring R is also very stiff: it stretches only 4.0 cm under that weight. Its ratio of mass to spring constant is the smallest.'
  D: 'Spring S stretches less than spring P, so its ratio of mass to spring constant, and its period, is smaller.'
---

The period of a block on a spring is $T = 2\pi\sqrt{m/k}$. You do not know each $k$, but the stretch tells you the ratio $m/k$.

At rest, the spring force balances the weight:

$$
k\,\Delta y = mg \quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{m}{k} = \frac{\Delta y}{g}
$$

So

$$
T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{\Delta y}{g}}
$$

The period depends only on how far the spring stretches at rest. The masses do not need to be compared at all. Spring P stretches the most (8.0 cm), so its block has the longest period.
