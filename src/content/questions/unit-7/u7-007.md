stem: |
  The graph shows part of the data from a motion sensor tracking a cart that
  oscillates on a spring. What is the frequency of the cart's oscillation?
choices:
  A: '0.80 Hz'
  B: '1.25 Hz'
  C: '2.5 Hz'
  D: '7.9 Hz'
answer: B
why_wrong:
  A: '0.80 is the period in seconds. The frequency is its reciprocal.'
  C: 'That is $1/(0.40\ \text{s})$, using the time from a peak to the next trough, which is only half a cycle.'
  D: 'That is the angular frequency, $2\pi f \approx 7.9$ rad/s. The frequency in hertz is the number of cycles per second, $1/T$.'
---

One full cycle runs from one peak to the next. The peaks are at $t = 0$ and $t = 0.80$ s, so the period is $T = 0.80$ s. (Peak to trough, 0.40 s, is only half a cycle.)

$$
f = \frac{1}{T} = \frac{1}{0.80\ \text{s}} = 1.25\ \text{Hz}
$$
