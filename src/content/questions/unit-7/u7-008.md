stem: |
  A 1.5 kg part in a machine is mounted on a spring and vibrates back and forth
  at 8.0 Hz. What is the spring constant of the spring, to two significant figures?
choices:
  A: '75 N/m'
  B: '600 N/m'
  C: '2500 N/m'
  D: '3800 N/m'
answer: D
why_wrong:
  A: 'That is $m\omega$. In $k = m\omega^2$ the angular frequency must be squared.'
  B: 'That squares only the frequency, giving $2\pi m f^2$. The whole angular frequency, $2\pi f$, must be squared.'
  C: 'That is $\omega^2$ by itself. It still needs to be multiplied by the mass, 1.5 kg.'
---

For a mass on a spring, $T = 2\pi\sqrt{m/k}$, which rearranges to

$$
k = m (2\pi f)^2
$$

Here $2\pi f = 2\pi(8.0\ \text{Hz}) \approx 50\ \text{rad/s}$, so

$$
k = (1.5\ \text{kg})(50.3\ \text{s}^{-1})^2 \approx 3800\ \text{N/m}
$$
