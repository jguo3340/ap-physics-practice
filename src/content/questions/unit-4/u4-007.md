stem: |
  Two carts of equal mass are joined by a stretchy cord on a level track. The
  graph shows the velocity of each cart over 5 s. What is the speed of the
  center of mass of the two-cart system at $t = 2.5$ s?
choices:
  A: '1.5 m/s'
  B: '2.0 m/s'
  C: '2.5 m/s'
  D: '4.0 m/s'
answer: B
why_wrong:
  A: 'That is the slower cart''s speed at 2.5 s. The center of mass moves at the mass-weighted average of both carts.'
  C: 'That is the faster cart''s speed at 2.5 s. The center of mass moves at the mass-weighted average of both carts.'
  D: 'This adds the two speeds. For the center of mass you divide the total momentum by the total mass, which here means averaging the speeds.'
---

The velocity of the center of mass is the total momentum divided by the total mass:

$$
v_{cm} = \frac{m_1 v_1 + m_2 v_2}{m_1 + m_2}
$$

With equal masses this is just the average of the two velocities. At $t = 2.5$ s the graph shows $2.5$ m/s and $1.5$ m/s:

$$
v_{cm} = \frac{2.5 + 1.5}{2} = 2.0\ \text{m/s}
$$

You get the same 2.0 m/s at any time on the graph, because no net external force acts on the system.
