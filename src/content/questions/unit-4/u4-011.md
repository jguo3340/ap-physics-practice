stem: |
  A 2.0 kg cart moving at 1.5 m/s and a 0.50 kg cart moving at 6.0 m/s roll
  toward each other on a level, frictionless track. They collide and stick
  together. What is the total kinetic energy of the carts just after the
  collision?
choices:
  A: '0 J'
  B: '2.25 J'
  C: '6.75 J'
  D: '11.25 J'
answer: A
why_wrong:
  B: 'That is the kinetic energy the 2.0 kg cart had before the collision. After it, the carts move together, and their common speed must be found from momentum.'
  C: 'This subtracts the two kinetic energies. Kinetic energy is not a vector; it cannot cancel by direction. Find the final speed from momentum instead.'
  D: 'That is the total kinetic energy before the collision. Carts that stick together always lose kinetic energy.'
---

Take the 2.0 kg cart's direction as positive. The total momentum before the collision is

$$
(2.0)(1.5) + (0.50)(-6.0) = 3.0 - 3.0 = 0
$$

Momentum is conserved, so after the carts stick together their total momentum is still zero, which means they are at rest. With no motion, the kinetic energy is $0$ J.

All 11.25 J of the original kinetic energy goes into deforming the carts, heat, and sound. Momentum can cancel by direction; kinetic energy cannot, so it is not conserved here.
