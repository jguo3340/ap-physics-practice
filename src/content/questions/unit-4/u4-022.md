stem: |
  A long cart is at rest on a level, frictionless track. A student sitting at
  the back of the cart throws a beanbag toward the front, where it is caught
  in a net attached to the cart. Which of the following describes the motion of
  the cart?
choices:
  A: 'The cart stays at rest the whole time, because the student, the beanbag, and the net are all part of the cart system.'
  B: 'The cart moves backward while the beanbag is in the air, and keeps moving backward after the catch.'
  C: 'The cart moves backward while the beanbag is in the air, then stops when the beanbag is caught.'
  D: 'The cart moves forward while the beanbag is in the air, then stops when the beanbag is caught.'
answer: C
why_wrong:
  A: 'While the beanbag is in the air it is not touching the cart. It carries forward momentum, so the rest of the system must carry the same amount backward.'
  B: 'The catch gives the cart a forward push equal to the backward push from the throw. Total momentum is zero, so once the beanbag is back on the cart, everything stops.'
  D: 'The student pushes the beanbag forward, so the beanbag pushes the student (and the cart) backward.'
---

Treat the cart, student, net, and beanbag as one system. There is no horizontal external force, and everything starts at rest, so the total horizontal momentum is zero the whole time.

- **During the throw:** the beanbag gets forward momentum. The cart and student must get the same amount of backward momentum, so they roll backward.
- **In the air:** the beanbag moves forward and the cart moves backward; the total stays zero.
- **The catch:** the beanbag ends up at rest on the cart. With the total still zero, the cart must stop too.

The center of mass never moves. The beanbag ends up farther forward, so the cart ends up a little farther back than where it started.
