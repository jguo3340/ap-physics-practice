stem: |
  A 5 kg cart moving east collides with a 2 kg cart moving west on a level,
  frictionless track. The carts stick together. Which of the following is true
  of the carts' motion just after the collision?
choices:
  A: 'They must be moving east.'
  B: 'They must be moving west.'
  C: 'They must be at rest.'
  D: 'Their motion cannot be determined without the speeds before the collision.'
answer: D
why_wrong:
  A: 'The heavier cart does not automatically win. If the 2 kg cart were moving fast enough, it could carry more momentum than the 5 kg cart.'
  B: 'The lighter cart would have to be moving much faster for that. Without the speeds, there is no way to know.'
  C: 'The carts stop only if their momenta are exactly equal and opposite, and the speeds are not given.'
---

The combined carts move in the direction of the **total momentum**:

$$
p_{\text{total}} = (5\ \text{kg})\,v_{\text{east}} - (2\ \text{kg})\,v_{\text{west}}
$$

Depending on the speeds, this can be positive, negative, or zero. For example:

- Both at 2 m/s: $10 - 4 = 6$, so the carts move east.
- East cart at 1 m/s, west cart at 4 m/s: $5 - 8 = -3$, so they move west.

Mass alone does not decide the outcome. Momentum depends on both mass and velocity.
