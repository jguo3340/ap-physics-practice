stem: |
  The graph shows the positions of two identical carts on a straight, level
  track as functions of time. Which statement about the magnitude of the
  momentum of the two-cart system, $p_{\text{sys}}$, is correct?
choices:
  A: '$p_{\text{sys}}$ is constant from 0 to 4 s.'
  B: '$p_{\text{sys}}$ is larger after $t = 2\ \text{s}$ than before.'
  C: '$p_{\text{sys}}$ is smaller after $t = 2\ \text{s}$ than before.'
  D: '$p_{\text{sys}}$ increases at a steady rate from 0 to 4 s.'
answer: B
why_wrong:
  A: 'Both carts change speed at 2 s, so the total momentum changes too. Momentum stays constant only if no outside force acts.'
  C: 'Read the slopes: both carts are faster after 2 s. Cart 1 goes from 0 to 1 m/s, and cart 2 goes from 0.5 to 1 m/s.'
  D: 'Each graph is made of straight pieces, so each cart''s speed is constant before and after 2 s. The momentum jumps at 2 s; it does not grow steadily.'
---

Each cart's velocity is the slope of its position graph. Call each cart's mass $m$.

**Before 2 s:**

- Cart 1 stays at 3 m, so $v_1 = 0$.
- Cart 2 goes from 0 to 1 m in 2 s, so $v_2 = 0.5\ \text{m/s}$.

$$
p_{\text{sys}} = m(0) + m(0.5) = 0.5m
$$

**After 2 s:**

- Cart 1 goes from 3 m to 5 m in 2 s, so $v_1 = 1\ \text{m/s}$.
- Cart 2 goes from 1 m to 3 m in 2 s, so $v_2 = 1\ \text{m/s}$.

$$
p_{\text{sys}} = m(1) + m(1) = 2m
$$

The system's momentum is four times larger after 2 s, so an outside force must have acted on the carts around that time.
