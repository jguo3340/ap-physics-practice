stem: |
  Carts X and Y roll toward each other on a level, frictionless track. Magnets
  in their bumpers make them repel before they touch. For one instant the carts
  move with the same velocity, and then they move apart. The table gives the
  data, with velocities in m/s and to the right as positive.

  $\begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c} \text{Cart} & \text{Mass} & \text{Before} & \text{Closest} & \text{After} \\ \hline X & 2.0\ \text{kg} & +4.0 & 0 & -4.0 \\ Y & ? & -2.0 & 0 & +2.0 \end{array}$

  Which question can be answered using only the data in the table?
choices:
  A: 'Which cart has the stronger magnet?'
  B: 'What is the mass of cart Y?'
  C: 'What is the largest force exerted on cart X?'
  D: 'How long do the carts push on each other?'
answer: B
why_wrong:
  A: 'By Newton''s third law, the magnets push on each other with equal forces, whatever their strengths. The motion data cannot single out one magnet.'
  C: 'Finding a force from a change in momentum needs a time. The table gives velocities but no times.'
  D: 'The data show how much each momentum changed, but not how quickly. A strong push for a short time and a weak push for a long time give the same change.'
---

Momentum is conserved, so the total momentum before equals the total after. Let $m$ be cart Y's mass.

$$
(2.0)(4.0) + m(-2.0) = (2.0)(-4.0) + m(2.0)
$$

$$
8.0 - 2.0m = -8.0 + 2.0m
$$

$$
m = 4.0\ \text{kg}
$$

The "closest" column agrees: the total momentum is $8.0 - 8.0 = 0$, so the common velocity is zero.

Questions about forces and times cannot be answered, because the table has no time information. With the mass found, you could also check whether kinetic energy was conserved: it is 24 J before and 24 J after.
