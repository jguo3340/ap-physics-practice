stem: |
  A uniform meterstick weighs 2 N. A 6 N object hangs from its 0 cm mark, and a
  4 N object hangs from its 80 cm mark. The meterstick hangs from a single
  string tied to the ceiling and stays horizontal and at rest. At what mark is
  the string tied?
choices:
  A: '27 cm'
  B: '32 cm'
  C: '35 cm'
  D: '40 cm'
answer: C
why_wrong:
  A: 'This includes the meterstick''s weight in the string''s tension but leaves out the torque from that weight, which acts at the 50 cm mark.'
  B: 'This ignores the meterstick completely. Its 2 N weight acts at the 50 cm mark and must be included in the balance.'
  D: 'This is halfway between the two objects. The objects have different weights, and the meterstick has weight too, so the balance point is not the midpoint.'
---

**Tension:** the meterstick is at rest, so the upward string force balances all the weights:

$$
T = 6 + 2 + 4 = 12\ \text{N}
$$

**Torques about the 0 cm end:** the 6 N object is at the pivot, so it has no torque about this point. The meterstick's weight acts at its center, 50 cm.

$$
T\,x = (2)(50) + (4)(80)
$$

$$
12x = 420 \quad\Rightarrow\quad x = 35\ \text{cm}
$$

**Check, with torques about the 35 cm mark:** counterclockwise, $(6)(35) = 210\ \text{N}\cdot\text{cm}$. Clockwise, $(2)(15) + (4)(45) = 30 + 180 = 210\ \text{N}\cdot\text{cm}$. They balance.
