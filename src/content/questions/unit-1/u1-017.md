stem: |
  The graph shows the position $x$ of a toy car moving along a straight track
  as a function of time $t$. The curve is part of a parabola, and it is flat
  (zero slope) at $t = 0$. Which of the following best describes the graph of
  the car's velocity $v$ as a function of time for the same interval?
choices:
  A: 'A horizontal line at a negative value'
  B: 'A straight line that starts at a negative value and rises to zero'
  C: 'A downward-opening parabola that starts at zero'
  D: 'A straight line that starts at zero and slopes downward'
answer: D
why_wrong:
  A: 'A constant velocity would make the position graph a straight line. This graph curves, so the velocity is changing.'
  B: 'This has the time order backward. The position graph is flat at $t = 0$ and steepest at the end, so the velocity starts at zero and gets more negative.'
  C: 'The velocity graph is the slope of the position graph. For a parabola, the slope changes at a steady rate, which gives a straight line, not another parabola.'
---

Velocity is the **slope** of the position–time graph. Read the slope at a few times:

- At $t = 0$ the curve is flat, so $v = 0$.
- After that the curve slopes downward, so $v$ is negative.
- The curve gets steeper as time goes on, so $v$ becomes more and more negative.

A parabola on a position graph means constant acceleration. Constant acceleration means the velocity changes by the same amount each second, which is a **straight line** on a velocity graph.

So the velocity graph is a straight line that starts at zero and slopes downward. Here $x = 8 - \tfrac12 t^2$ (in meters and seconds), so $v = -t$: the car speeds up in the negative direction at $1\ \text{m/s}^2$.
