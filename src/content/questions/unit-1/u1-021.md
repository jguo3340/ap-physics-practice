stem: |
  Two balls are launched from the same spot on level ground at the same time and
  with the same speed. Ball 1 is launched at $20^\circ$ above the horizontal, and
  ball 2 at $70^\circ$ above the horizontal. Air resistance is negligible. Graphs
  of each ball's vertical velocity $v_y$ against time $t$ are drawn on the same
  axes, with up as positive. Which statement about the graphs is correct?
choices:
  A: 'The lines are parallel, and ball 2''s line starts higher and reaches zero later.'
  B: 'The lines start at the same value, and ball 2''s line is steeper.'
  C: 'The lines are parallel and start at the same value, so they lie on top of each other.'
  D: 'Ball 1''s line is steeper, because ball 1 moves faster horizontally.'
answer: A
why_wrong:
  B: 'The launch speeds are equal, but their vertical parts are not. Ball 2 is aimed more steeply, so it starts with a larger $v_y$. And both slopes are $-g$.'
  C: 'The slopes are both $-g$, but the starting values differ: $v_0 \sin 70^\circ$ is larger than $v_0 \sin 20^\circ$.'
  D: 'Horizontal motion has no effect on vertical motion. The slope of each $v_y$ graph is the vertical acceleration, which is $-g$ for both balls.'
---

The slope of a $v_y$–$t$ graph is the vertical acceleration. With no air resistance, both balls have acceleration $-g$, so **both lines have slope $-g$**: they are parallel.

The starting values are the vertical parts of the launch velocity:

$$
v_{1y} = v_0 \sin 20^\circ \approx 0.34\,v_0
$$

$$
v_{2y} = v_0 \sin 70^\circ \approx 0.94\,v_0
$$

So ball 2's line starts higher. With the same slope, it takes longer to come down to zero, which matches ball 2 rising higher and reaching its peak later.
