stem: |
  The graph shows the velocity of an object moving in a straight line. What is
  the object's displacement from $t = 0$ to $t = 6$ s?
choices:
  A: '4 m'
  B: '16 m'
  C: '20 m'
  D: '24 m'
answer: C
why_wrong:
  A: 'That is only the area from 0 to 2 s. The object keeps moving from 2 s to 6 s.'
  B: 'That is only the area from 2 s to 6 s. The triangle from 0 to 2 s counts too.'
  D: 'That is either 4 m/s × 6 s, which treats the object as moving at 4 m/s from the start, or the area all the way to 8 s.'
---

On a velocity–time graph, the displacement is the area between the line and the time axis.

- From 0 to 2 s the area is a triangle: $\tfrac12 (2\ \text{s})(4\ \text{m/s}) = 4\ \text{m}$.
- From 2 s to 6 s it is a rectangle: $(4\ \text{s})(4\ \text{m/s}) = 16\ \text{m}$.

Together, the displacement is $4\ \text{m} + 16\ \text{m} = 20\ \text{m}$.
