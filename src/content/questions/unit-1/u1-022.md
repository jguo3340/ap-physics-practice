stem: |
  A student walks back and forth along a straight hallway in front of a motion
  detector. The graph shows the student's position $x$ as a function of time
  $t$ from 0 to 8 s. Which statement about the student's motion during this
  interval is correct?
choices:
  A: 'The distance the student travels is 1.5 m.'
  B: 'The student''s velocity is negative for the whole time from 2 s to 8 s.'
  C: 'The student''s average speed equals the magnitude of the average velocity.'
  D: 'The student''s velocity is zero at exactly two instants.'
answer: D
why_wrong:
  A: 'That is the displacement, from 0 to 1.5 m. The student walks out 3 m, back 4 m, and out again 2.5 m, a total distance of 9.5 m.'
  B: 'The graph slopes downward only from 2 s to 5 s. After 5 s it slopes upward, so the velocity is positive.'
  C: 'Average speed uses the total distance (9.5 m), while average velocity uses the displacement (1.5 m). They are equal only if the student never turns around.'
---

Velocity is the slope of the position graph, so read the slope:

- 0 to 2 s: the curve rises, so $v > 0$.
- At 2 s: the curve is at a peak and flat, so $v = 0$.
- 2 to 5 s: the curve falls, so $v < 0$.
- At 5 s: the curve is at a low point and flat, so $v = 0$.
- 5 to 8 s: the curve rises again, so $v > 0$. It is still rising at 8 s.

The slope is zero only at the peak (2 s) and the low point (5 s). Those are the two moments the student turns around.

For the other choices, the distance is $3 + 4 + 2.5 = 9.5\ \text{m}$, while the displacement is only $1.5\ \text{m}$. Average speed is $9.5/8 \approx 1.2\ \text{m/s}$, but the magnitude of average velocity is $1.5/8 \approx 0.19\ \text{m/s}$.
