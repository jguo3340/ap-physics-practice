stem: |
  The graph shows the velocity $v$ of an object moving along a straight line
  from $t = 0$ to $t = 10$ s. Which of the following statements about the
  object's motion is true?
choices:
  A: 'The object is speeding up from $t = 3$ s to $t = 4$ s.'
  B: 'The object is at rest from $t = 4$ s to $t = 8$ s.'
  C: 'The object is back at its starting position at $t = 4$ s.'
  D: 'The acceleration from $t = 2$ s to $t = 4$ s points the same way as the acceleration from $t = 8$ s to $t = 10$ s.'
answer: A
why_wrong:
  B: 'A flat line on a velocity graph means constant velocity, not zero velocity. From 4 s to 8 s the object moves at a steady 4 m/s in the negative direction.'
  C: 'The area from 0 to 2 s is +8 m, and the areas from 2 s to 3 s and 3 s to 4 s cancel. At 4 s the object is still 8 m from where it started.'
  D: 'From 2 s to 4 s the velocity decreases, so the acceleration is negative. From 8 s to 10 s it increases toward zero, so the acceleration is positive. They point opposite ways.'
---

Speed is the size of the velocity, ignoring its sign. From 3 s to 4 s the velocity goes from 0 to $-4$ m/s, so the speed goes from 0 to 4 m/s: the object is speeding up, just in the negative direction.

A quick test: an object speeds up when its velocity and acceleration have the same sign. From 3 s to 4 s the velocity is negative and the slope (the acceleration) is negative too.

Checking the others:

- **4 s to 8 s:** the line is flat at $-4$ m/s, so the object moves steadily, not at rest.
- **Position at 4 s:** the area from 0 to 2 s is $(2\ \text{s})(4\ \text{m/s}) = 8$ m. The two triangles from 2 s to 4 s are the same size, one above the axis and one below, so they cancel. The object is 8 m from its start.
- **Accelerations:** the slope is $-4\ \text{m/s}^2$ from 2 s to 4 s and $+2\ \text{m/s}^2$ from 8 s to 10 s, opposite signs.
