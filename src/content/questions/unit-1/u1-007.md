stem: |
  The graph shows the position $x$ of a cart moving along a straight track. The
  curve is a parabola with its highest point at $t = 2$ s. Which statement about
  the cart at $t = 1$ s and at $t = 3$ s is correct?
choices:
  A: 'Its velocity is the same at both times.'
  B: 'Its speed is the same at both times, but its acceleration points in opposite directions at the two times.'
  C: 'It is slowing down at both times.'
  D: 'Its speed is the same at both times, and its acceleration is the same at both times.'
answer: D
why_wrong:
  A: 'The graph is just as steep at both times, but it slopes up at 1 s and down at 3 s. The cart moves in the +x direction at 1 s and in the −x direction at 3 s, so the velocities point opposite ways.'
  B: 'A parabola on a position–time graph means the acceleration is constant. The cart turns around at 2 s, but its acceleration does not flip: it points in the −x direction the whole time.'
  C: 'At 1 s the velocity is +x and the acceleration is −x, so the cart is slowing down. At 3 s both point in the −x direction, so the cart is speeding up.'
---

**Velocity is the slope** of a position–time graph.

- At 1 s the graph slopes up, so the cart moves in the +x direction.
- At 3 s it slopes down just as steeply (the parabola is symmetric about 2 s), so the cart moves in the −x direction at the same speed.

**Acceleration is how the slope changes.** A parabola means the slope changes at a steady rate, so the acceleration is constant. This curve bends downward everywhere, so the acceleration points in the −x direction at every moment, including at 2 s, where the cart is momentarily at rest.

That makes the speed the same and the acceleration the same at both times. The only difference is the direction of motion: at 1 s the cart is slowing down (velocity and acceleration opposite), and at 3 s it is speeding up (velocity and acceleration the same way).

For the numbers: from rest at 2 s the cart moves 8 m back in 2 s, so $-8\ \text{m} = \tfrac12 a (2\ \text{s})^2$ gives $a = -4\ \text{m/s}^2$. The velocity is $+4$ m/s at 1 s and $-4$ m/s at 3 s.
