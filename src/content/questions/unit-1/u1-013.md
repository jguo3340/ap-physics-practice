stem: |
  The graph shows the velocity of a cart moving along a straight track. The
  motion has three segments, labeled 1, 2, and 3. Which of the following
  correctly ranks the cart's displacements $\Delta x_1$, $\Delta x_2$, and
  $\Delta x_3$ during the three segments?
choices:
  A: '$\Delta x_1 > \Delta x_2 > \Delta x_3 > 0$'
  B: '$\Delta x_1 = \Delta x_2 > \Delta x_3 > 0$'
  C: '$\Delta x_2 > \Delta x_1 > \Delta x_3 = 0$'
  D: '$\Delta x_1 = \Delta x_2 > 0 > \Delta x_3$'
answer: D
why_wrong:
  A: 'Speeding up and slowing down don''t matter here. Segments 1 and 2 are triangles with the same base and height, so their areas are equal.'
  B: 'Segment 3 is below the time axis. The cart is moving backward then, so its displacement is negative, not positive.'
  C: 'Segment 3 starts at zero velocity, but the cart is moving for the whole segment. Its area is not zero.'
---

On a velocity–time graph, the displacement during a time interval is the area between the line and the time axis. Area above the axis is positive and area below it is negative.

- Segment 1 (0 to 2 s): a triangle, $\tfrac12 (2\ \text{s})(6\ \text{m/s}) = +6$ m.
- Segment 2 (2 to 4 s): the same triangle flipped, $\tfrac12 (2\ \text{s})(6\ \text{m/s}) = +6$ m.
- Segment 3 (4 to 6 s): below the axis, $-\tfrac12 (2\ \text{s})(3\ \text{m/s}) = -3$ m.

So $\Delta x_1 = \Delta x_2 = 6\ \text{m} > 0 > \Delta x_3 = -3\ \text{m}$.
