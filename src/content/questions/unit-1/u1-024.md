stem: |
  At $t = 0$ a cart is at $x = -4\ \text{m}$ and is moving at $5\ \text{m/s}$ in
  the $+x$ direction. The cart has a constant acceleration in the $-x$
  direction, and the size of the acceleration is somewhere between
  $2\ \text{m/s}^2$ and $4\ \text{m/s}^2$. Which range contains the cart's
  position at $t = 2\ \text{s}$?
choices:
  A: '$-10\ \text{m} < x < -2\ \text{m}$'
  B: '$-2\ \text{m} < x < 2\ \text{m}$'
  C: '$2\ \text{m} < x < 6\ \text{m}$'
  D: '$10\ \text{m} < x < 14\ \text{m}$'
answer: B
why_wrong:
  A: 'This leaves out the $\tfrac12$ in $\tfrac12 a t^2$, which doubles the effect of the acceleration.'
  C: 'This is the right displacement range, but it forgets that the cart started at $x = -4\ \text{m}$, not at the origin.'
  D: 'This treats the acceleration as pointing in the $+x$ direction. It points in $-x$, so it subtracts from the position.'
---

Use $x = x_0 + v_0 t + \tfrac12 a t^2$ with $x_0 = -4\ \text{m}$, $v_0 = +5\ \text{m/s}$, $t = 2\ \text{s}$, and a negative $a$:

$$
x = -4 + (5)(2) + \tfrac12 a (2)^2 = 6 + 2a
$$

The acceleration is between $-4$ and $-2\ \text{m/s}^2$:

$$
a = -2: \quad x = 6 - 4 = 2\ \text{m}
$$

$$
a = -4: \quad x = 6 - 8 = -2\ \text{m}
$$

So the position is between $-2\ \text{m}$ and $2\ \text{m}$.
