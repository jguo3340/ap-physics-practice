stem: |
  Puck A, of mass $m$, slides at speed $v$ across frictionless ice toward an
  identical puck B, which is at rest and tied to a fixed post by a light string
  of length $R$. Puck A's path is perpendicular to the string. The pucks collide
  head-on, and afterward puck A is at rest. Knowing which of the following
  allows you to find the angular impulse on puck B about the post due to the
  collision?
choices:
  A: '$m$, $v$, and $R$'
  B: '$v$ and $R$ only'
  C: '$m$, $v$, and how long the collision lasts'
  D: '$m$ and $v$ only'
answer: A
why_wrong:
  B: 'Angular momentum, $L = Rmv$, depends on the puck''s mass as well as its speed and distance from the post.'
  C: 'The duration of the collision isn''t needed. The angular impulse equals the change in angular momentum, which you can find from the motion before and after.'
  D: 'Those give the linear impulse, $mv$. The angular impulse about the post also depends on how far from the post the puck moves: $R$.'
---

Angular impulse equals change in angular momentum, so find puck B's angular momentum about the post before and after.

- **Before:** B is at rest, so $L = 0$.
- **After:** A stops, so by conservation of momentum, B (identical to A) moves off at speed $v$, perpendicular to the string. Its angular momentum about the post is $L = Rmv$.

So the angular impulse is $\Delta L = Rmv$, which needs $m$, $v$, and $R$.
