stem: |
  A box slides down a rough ramp at a constant speed. Consider the system made
  of the box, the ramp, and Earth. Which of the following best describes the
  energy transformation in the system?
choices:
  A: 'Kinetic energy is turned into thermal energy.'
  B: 'Gravitational potential energy is turned into kinetic energy.'
  C: 'No energy is transformed, because the box''s speed is constant.'
  D: 'Gravitational potential energy is turned into thermal energy.'
answer: D
why_wrong:
  A: 'The box''s speed is constant, so its kinetic energy is not decreasing. Nothing is being taken from kinetic energy.'
  B: 'If potential energy became kinetic energy, the box would speed up. Its speed is constant.'
  C: 'The box is getting lower, so gravitational potential energy is decreasing. That energy has to go somewhere.'
---

Track each kind of energy:

- **Kinetic:** constant speed, so no change.
- **Gravitational potential:** the box gets lower, so it decreases.
- **Thermal:** the box and ramp rub and warm up, so it increases.

The total energy of the system stays the same, so the lost potential energy becomes thermal energy. Friction is inside this system, which is why it shows up as thermal energy rather than as work done from outside.
