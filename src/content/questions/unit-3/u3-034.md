stem: |
  An object hangs at rest from a spring attached to the ceiling at point S. The
  object's equilibrium position E is a distance $d$ below S. The object is then
  held at one of the labeled points P, Q, or R, so that the spring stretches in
  a straight line from S to that point. Which of the following correctly ranks
  the elastic potential energy $U$ stored in the spring when the object is at
  each point?
choices:
  A: '$U_R > U_Q > U_P$'
  B: '$U_Q = U_R > U_P$'
  C: '$U_R > U_P > U_Q$'
  D: '$U_P = U_R > U_Q$'
answer: A
why_wrong:
  B: 'This counts only how far each point is below E. The spring''s energy depends on its total length, and sideways distance also makes the spring longer.'
  C: 'This ranks the points by their distance from E. What matters is the distance from the spring''s fixed end, S.'
  D: 'This counts only how far each point is to the side of E. Moving straight down also stretches the spring.'
---

The energy stored in a spring depends on how far it is stretched beyond its natural length, $U = \tfrac12 k (\Delta L)^2$. The spring already stretches at E, so its natural length is less than $d$. A longer spring means a bigger stretch and more energy.

The spring runs straight from S to the object, so find each point's distance from S:

$$
P:\ \sqrt{d^2 + d^2} \approx 1.41d
$$

$$
Q:\ d + \tfrac12 d = 1.50d
$$

$$
R:\ \sqrt{d^2 + (1.5d)^2} \approx 1.80d
$$

The spring is longest at R and shortest at P, so $U_R > U_Q > U_P$.
