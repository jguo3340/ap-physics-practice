stem: |
  Two skateboarders, one with a total mass of 50 kg and the other 75 kg, roll
  at the same speed toward two different ramps. One ramp is steep and the
  other is gentle. Friction and air resistance are negligible. How do the
  greatest heights the two skateboarders reach compare?
choices:
  A: 'The 75 kg skateboarder rises higher, because more mass means more kinetic energy.'
  B: 'They reach the same height.'
  C: 'The 50 kg skateboarder rises higher, because gravity pulls less on a smaller mass.'
  D: 'The skateboarder on the gentle ramp rises higher, because that ramp is longer.'
answer: B
why_wrong:
  A: 'The heavier skateboarder does have more kinetic energy, but also needs proportionally more energy to rise each meter. The two effects cancel.'
  C: 'Gravity does pull less on the smaller mass, but that skateboarder also has proportionally less kinetic energy to spend. The two effects cancel.'
  D: 'The gentle ramp lets the skateboarder roll farther along the ramp, but not higher. Without friction, the height depends only on the starting speed.'
---

For each skateboarder–Earth system, mechanical energy is conserved. All of the kinetic energy at the bottom becomes gravitational potential energy at the highest point:

$$
\tfrac12 m v^2 = m g h \quad\Rightarrow\quad h = \frac{v^2}{2g}
$$

The mass cancels, and the shape of the ramp never appears. Both skateboarders start with the same speed, so both reach the same height.
