stem: |
  The graph shows the potential energy $U$ of a system as one object moves along
  the $x$-axis while the rest of the system stays put. The object passes point P
  moving in the $+x$ direction, and the system's mechanical energy stays 4.0 J
  the whole time. Which of the following describes the magnitude of the object's
  momentum in segment QR and in segment RS?
choices:
  A: 'QR: zero. RS: increasing.'
  B: 'QR: constant but not zero. RS: increasing.'
  C: 'QR: constant but not zero. RS: decreasing.'
  D: 'QR: zero. RS: decreasing.'
answer: B
why_wrong:
  A: 'A flat $U$ graph means zero force, not zero speed. The object still has $4.0 - 3.0 = 1.0\ \text{J}$ of kinetic energy in QR, so it keeps moving.'
  C: 'From R to S the potential energy drops, so the kinetic energy rises. More kinetic energy means more speed and more momentum.'
  D: 'The object is not at rest in QR, because its kinetic energy there is 1.0 J. And its kinetic energy grows in RS, not shrinks.'
---

The kinetic energy is whatever is left of the 4.0 J total after the potential energy:

$$
K = E - U = 4.0\ \text{J} - U
$$

**Segment QR:** $U$ stays at 3.0 J, so $K$ stays at 1.0 J. The speed is constant and not zero, so the momentum is constant and not zero.

**Segment RS:** $U$ drops from 3.0 J to 0, so $K$ rises from 1.0 J to 4.0 J. The speed increases, and so does the momentum $p = mv$.
