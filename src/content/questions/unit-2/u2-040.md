stem: |
  Three crates sit in a row on a rough floor, touching each other. A worker
  pushes horizontally on crate 1 with force $P$, and all three crates slide
  forward together. Which list gives every force exerted on crate 2, the middle
  crate?
choices:
  A: 'Gravity, the floor''s normal force, a push from crate 1, a push from crate 3, and friction from the floor'
  B: 'Gravity, the floor''s normal force, the push $P$, and friction from the floor'
  C: 'Gravity, the floor''s normal force, a push from crate 1, and friction from the floor'
  D: 'Gravity, the floor''s normal force, the push $P$, a push from crate 1, and friction from the floor'
answer: A
why_wrong:
  B: 'The worker touches only crate 1, so $P$ is not a force on crate 2. Crate 2 is pushed by crate 1, which is touching it.'
  C: 'Crate 2 pushes forward on crate 3, so by the third law crate 3 pushes back on crate 2. That backward push belongs on the list.'
  D: 'The push $P$ acts on crate 1, not crate 2. Listing it here counts the same push twice.'
---

List only forces exerted **on** crate 2, each by something that touches it (plus gravity, which acts at a distance).

- **Gravity** from Earth, down.
- **Normal force** from the floor, up.
- **Friction** from the floor, backward, because the crate slides forward over a rough floor.
- **Crate 1** touches crate 2 from behind and pushes it forward.
- **Crate 3** touches crate 2 from the front. Crate 2 pushes crate 3 forward, so crate 3 pushes crate 2 backward.

The worker's push $P$ is not on the list, because the worker does not touch crate 2.
